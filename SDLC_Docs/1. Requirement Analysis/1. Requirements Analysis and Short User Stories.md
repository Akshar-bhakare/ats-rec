### **1. UltraAdmin**

**Who are they?**
- Superuser with global visibility and system-wide privileges (product owner, platform IT, or SaaS operator).

**Primary Goals:**
- Onboard/manage clients (tenants).
- Configure system-wide integrations (telephony, OpenAI, communication).
- Monitor usage, credits, critical alerts, and system health.
- Enforce platform policies and compliance.
- Respond to emergencies (system pause, maintenance, security).

**Key Actions/Workflows:**
- Add, suspend, or delete clients.
- Allocate credits, set API rate limits.
- Access all clients’ dashboards, audit logs, and usage analytics.
- Manage global template and communication libraries.
- Oversee emergency controls, perform global maintenance.

**Pain Points:**
- Need for instant visibility into platform health and incidents.
- Ability to enforce compliance and data privacy at scale.
- Must respond quickly to outages or API/credit overages.

---

### **2. ClientAdmin**

**Who are they?**
- Admin of a specific company/client (HR/TA lead, Talent Ops, client-side platform owner).

**Primary Goals:**
- Set up and manage their company’s instance.
- Add/manage recruiters, assign roles, configure company info.
- Approve/review jobs and pipelines.
- Manage communication templates and workflows.
- Monitor team activity, recruiter performance, and credits.

**Key Actions/Workflows:**
- Create and manage jobs, pipelines, company settings.
- Onboard recruiters (send invites, set permissions).
- Edit communication and feedback templates.
- Monitor credits, performance dashboards, export reports.
- Assign jobs to agencies (control which agencies can submit).

**Pain Points:**
- Streamlining onboarding for many recruiters.
- Quick, clear status of jobs, candidates, and credits.
- Customizing workflows to fit company policies.

---

### **3. Recruiter**

**Who are they?**
- User working for a client, responsible for finding and managing candidates (internal or agency-side).

**Primary Goals:**
- Post jobs, manage candidate pipelines.
- Search, add, screen, and move candidates.
- Schedule and review interviews (AI/manual), communicate with candidates.
- Track progress, update statuses, log feedback.

**Key Actions/Workflows:**
- Search/add candidates (manual, bulk import, referrals).
- Initiate AI/telephony campaigns, schedule interviews.
- Review AI/manual interview results, add feedback.
- Communicate via WhatsApp, email, or phone from platform.
- Export/share candidate shortlists with hiring managers or clients.

**Pain Points:**
- Large volume management—need quick search, filters, status visibility.
- Reducing time-to-fill and communication overhead.
- Avoiding duplicated effort (e.g., duplicate candidates or interviews).

---

### **4. Agency**

**Who are they?**
- Third-party staffing provider; can submit candidates on behalf of clients, but only see their own candidates and submission statuses.

**Primary Goals:**
- Upload or import candidates for assigned jobs/clients.
- Track the status of their submissions—pending, interviewed, selected, rejected, placed.
- View feedback/responses on their submitted candidates.
- Access only their own data—no visibility into client internals beyond assigned jobs and statuses.

**Key Actions/Workflows:**
- Add candidate submissions (single or bulk) to open roles.
- Track submission status; get notified of progress or required actions.
- Withdraw or update submissions as needed.
- Download feedback or reports (placement stats, conversion rates).

**Pain Points:**
- Only want to see *their* candidates and submissions, not other agency/client data.
- Need clear status visibility—avoid confusion or lost updates.
- Bulk upload and mapping must be simple.

---

### **5. Candidate**

**Who are they?**
- Job seekers (external or referred via agency/recruiter).

**Primary Goals:**
- Register, complete their profile, upload resume, apply to jobs.
- Track application/interview progress.
- Receive timely communications, reminders, and feedback.
- Participate in AI/manual interviews and assessments.

**Key Actions/Workflows:**
- Register (direct, invited, or via agency link).
- Complete profile, upload documents, set preferences.
- Search/apply to jobs, track status via dashboard.
- Respond to interview invitations, complete assessments.
- Review feedback, next steps, or offers.

**Pain Points:**
- Need a smooth, mobile-friendly registration and application flow.
- Want instant updates about status (selected, rejected, interview scheduled).
- Concerned about privacy, data use, and receiving too many communications.

---

### **6. Interviewer (Panelist)**

**Who They Are:**

- A domain expert, hiring manager, or technical specialist **from the client’s organization**, invited for specific candidate interviews. They do **not** manage jobs/candidates in the system and typically have access only to interviews assigned to them.

**Primary Goals:**

- Conduct interviews (live/video/AI-assisted) for open positions.
- Evaluate candidates based on structured feedback forms.
- Access candidate details (resume, job applied for) only for their assigned interviews.
- Submit interview feedback promptly and securely.

**Key Actions/Workflows:**

- Logs in only when assigned to an interview.
- Provides honest, structured feedback.
- Has no access to wider system data—only to interviews/candidates assigned.

**Pain Points:**

- Too much data or system complexity.
- Trouble accessing interview or feedback forms.
- Not enough info about the candidate/role.
